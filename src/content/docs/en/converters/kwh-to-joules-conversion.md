One kilowatt-hour (kWh) equals 3 600 000 joules (J), i.e., 3.6 × 10⁶ J. This exact relationship is used in engineering and physics to convert electrical energy measurements to the fundamental unit of the International System, facilitating consumption calculations and comparison with other forms of energy.

## Conversion Formula
The conversion from kilowatt-hours to joules is performed using the following formula, where the conversion factor is constant and invariable:

> **E(J) = E(kWh) × 3.6 × 10⁶**

| Variable | Description | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E(J) | Energy expressed in joules | J |
| E(kWh) | Energy expressed in kilowatt-hours | kWh |
| 3.6 × 10⁶ | Conversion factor (joules per kilowatt-hour) | J/kWh |

## Definition and Equivalences
The kilowatt-hour (kWh) is a unit of energy corresponding to the power of one kilowatt (1000 W) applied for one hour. The joule (J) is the basic energy unit in the International System, equivalent to the work done by a force of one newton moving one meter. The main equivalences of 1 kWh in different units are shown below, always expressed with a metric and an imperial equivalent.

## Calculation Example
We want to convert 2.5 kWh to joules. Applying the formula:

> **E(J) = 2.5 kWh × 3.6 × 10⁶ J/kWh**

The result is **9 000 000 J** (9 × 10⁶ J), which is approximately **6 638 239.8 ft·lbf**. This value represents the energy consumed by a 2500 W equipment running for one hour.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
### What is the exact equivalence between kWh and joules?
One kilowatt-hour equals exactly 3 600 000 joules (3.6 × 10⁶ J). This relationship is fixed and derives from the definition of the joule as one watt-second.

### How do I convert kilowatt-hours to joules using the formula?
Multiply the amount of kWh by 3 600 000 to obtain the energy in joules. For example, 2 kWh becomes 7 200 000 J.

### Why does 1 kWh equal 3.6 million joules?
Because 1 kWh = 1000 W × 3600 s = 3 600 000 W·s, and the joule is, by definition, exactly one watt-second (1 J = 1 W·s).

### What is the equivalence of kWh in other energy units?
One kilowatt-hour equals 3 600 000 J / 2 655 295.92 ft·lbf, 3.6 MJ, 859.85 kcal / 3 412.14 BTU, among others. The equivalence table expands these values.

### Can joules be converted to kWh using the same formula?
Yes, the inverse formula is E(kWh) = E(J) ÷ 3 600 000. For example, 9 000 000 J equals 2.5 kWh.

### In what contexts is this conversion used?
It is applied in electric billing, where energy is measured in kWh, and in engineering calculations to transform electrical consumption into thermal or mechanical quantities, such as heat generated in a heating system.
