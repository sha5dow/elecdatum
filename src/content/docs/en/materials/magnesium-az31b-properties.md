## Introduction
Magnesium AZ31B is a lightweight magnesium alloy with a density of 1.77 g/cm³ and an excellent strength-to-weight ratio, used as an alternative to aluminum alloys in numerous applications. It is available in plate, sheet, and bar forms, and its availability is greater than that of other magnesium grades. To improve its corrosion resistance, it is usually subjected to anodizing.

## Chemical composition
The nominal aluminum content in AZ31B ranges from 2.50 % to 3.50 %, while zinc is maintained in the range 0.60 % – 1.40 %. The following table details the chemical composition according to ASTM specifications.

| Element | Content (%) |

| Magnesium, Mg | 97 |

## Physical properties
The typical density of AZ31B in extruded condition is 1.77 g/cm³ (1770 kg/m³). This is one of the lightest structural metal alloys commercially available.

| Property | Metric | Imperial |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Density | 1.77 g/cm³ | 0.0639 lb/in³ |

## Mechanical properties
The tensile strength of AZ31B in as-extruded condition (F) reaches 260 MPa. The following values correspond to the representative mechanical properties of the alloy in that condition.

## Thermal properties
The coefficient of linear thermal expansion between 0 °C and 100 °C is 26 µm/m·°C, and thermal conductivity reaches 96 W/m·K, which favors heat dissipation in components that require it.

## Machining
The AZ31B alloy has good machinability, although it must always be worked with abundant lubrication and a specific fire extinguishing system for magnesium, since fine chips can ignite. Constant supervision is required during the process.

## Forming
Forming of the alloy is carried out after preheating to 260 °C (500 °F). At this temperature the material acquires sufficient ductility to allow bending, deep drawing, or forging operations without cracking.

## Welding
The recommended welding techniques for AZ31B are shielded metal arc welding and gas tungsten arc welding (TIG). Proper joint preparation and removal of surface oxides are essential to obtain sound welds.

## Annealing
Full annealing is performed by heating the part to 344 °C (650 °F) and allowing it to cool slowly inside the furnace. For stress relief, a treatment of 30 to 60 minutes at 149 °C (300 °F) is applied, followed by air cooling.

## Applications
AZ31B is widely used in the aerospace and transportation industries due to its low weight and good strength. It also appears in electronic components, camera structures, housings for portable equipment, and parts formed by extrusion or deep drawing that require high specific stiffness.

## Comparison with other materials
AZ31B has a density 35 % lower than that of aluminum alloy 6061‑T6 (1.77 vs. 2.70 g/cm³), while its tensile strength is about 60 % of the latter, resulting in a comparable specific strength. Compared to other magnesium alloys such as AZ91 (9 % Al), AZ31B offers better formability and weldability, but lower strength and hardness. The equivalents to European standards identify it as EN‑MAMgAl3Zn1 and with the traditional designation MgAl3Zn1, useful for exchanges with suppliers in the European context.
