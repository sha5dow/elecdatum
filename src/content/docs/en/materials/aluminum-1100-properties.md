## General Description
Aluminum 1100 is a commercially pure alloy from the 1000 series with a minimum aluminum content of 99.0%. It exhibits excellent corrosion resistance, high electrical and thermal conductivity, and outstanding formability. It is not precipitation heat treatable, so its mechanical properties are improved by cold working (strain hardening). It is used in sectors requiring ductility and chemical stability, in the form of sheets, tubes, and extruded profiles.

## Heat Treatments and Tempers
Aluminum 1100 is not susceptible to age hardening. Its tempers are obtained exclusively by cold plastic deformation or annealing:
- **O (annealed):** Heated to 345 °C and cooled in air or water. Provides maximum ductility.
- **H12, H14, H18:** Increasing strain-hardened tempers (¼ hard, ½ hard, hard) achieved by cold working. The higher the number, the higher the strength and the lower the ductility.
- **H112:** Strain hardening obtained in hot forming processes with broad mechanical requirements.
The alloy does not accept T temper (solution and aging).

## Weldability and Forming
Alloy 1100 exhibits excellent weldability by all conventional processes (TIG, MIG, resistance, oxyacetylene, and brazing). No preheating is required and hot cracking does not occur.

Cold formability is very good, especially in O temper or soft tempers. In high strain-hardened tempers (H18) the bending capacity is reduced.

Machinability is poor in the annealed condition, but improves significantly in hard tempers; the use of carbide tools with oil lubrication is recommended for deep cuts.

## Corrosion Resistance
Aluminum 1100 offers excellent resistance to atmospheric corrosion, in contact with fresh water, and in the presence of many chemical agents. This resistance is due to the spontaneous formation of a protective oxide layer. It has no risk of stress corrosion cracking. It is suitable for food contact.

## Applications by Industry
- **Food and pharmaceutical:** processing equipment, tanks, trays, pipes.
- **Chemical:** heat exchangers, coils, pressure vessels.
- **Construction and decoration:** reflectors, nameplates, architectural cladding.
- **Electrical:** busbars, connectors, cables.
- **General industry:** deep-drawn parts, rivets, reflective materials.
- **Cryogenics:** components that retain ductility at sub-zero temperatures.
