## Summary
C260 brass (UNS C26000) is a copper-zinc alloy with approximately 70 % copper and 30 % zinc. Known as cartridge brass, it exhibits the highest ductility of the yellow brass family and stands out for its excellent cold formability, good corrosion resistance, and golden appearance. It is widely used in the manufacture of cartridges, architectural components, musical instruments, and plumbing applications.

## Fabrication and heat treatment

- **Annealing:** heat between 427 and 760 °C (800 – 1400 °F), followed by slow cooling to obtain maximum softness and ductility.
- **Cold working:** excellent suitability for deep drawing, bending, stamping, and coining. Work hardening is controlled with intermediate anneals.
- **Hot working:** forging is performed between 650 and 800 °C (1200 – 1470 °F). It is also suitable for extrusion and hot rolling.
- **Welding:** brazing, soldering, and oxyacetylene welding processes are recommended. Resistance welding is not advised.
- **Machining:** its machinability is moderate (machinability index ~20 % relative to free-cutting brass C360). Use of sharp tools and coolant is advisable in complex operations.
- **Finishing:** accepts all types of electroplated coatings (nickel, chrome, silver, gold) and protective lacquers.

## Applications by industry

- **Ammunition and defense:** cartridge cases and ammunition (hence the name "cartridge brass").
- **Architecture and decoration:** door handles, handrails, decorative frames, lighting fixtures, commemorative plaques.
- **Plumbing and gas fitting:** threaded fittings, valves, connections, faucets.
- **Electricity and electronics:** connection terminals, stamped connectors, low-load contacts.
- **Automotive and HVAC:** radiators, heater cores, coolant tanks, thermostats.
- **Musical instruments:** trumpet bodies, trombones, saxophones, cymbals, and bells.
- **Consumer goods:** zippers, jewelry, medals, engraved plates, lighters.

## Frequently asked questions (FAQ)

- **What is the maximum tensile strength of C260 brass?**  
  The maximum value reaches 896 MPa (130 ksi) in spring temper, although in the annealed condition it is only 303 MPa.

- **At what temperature does C260 brass melt?**  
  Melting begins at 916 °C (solidus) and completes at 954 °C (liquidus), corresponding to a melting range of 38 °C.

- **What is the exact density of this alloy?**  
  At 20 °C the density is 8.53 g/cm³, i.e., 8530 kg/m³ in SI units.

- **What elongation does C260 exhibit in the annealed condition?**  
  It reaches an elongation of 66 % in a 75 mm gauge length, demonstrating its exceptional plastic deformation capacity.

- **What is the thermal conductivity of C260 brass?**  
  Its thermal conductivity is 120 W/m·K at 20 °C, facilitating heat dissipation in electrical and thermal components.

- **Which ASTM standard covers C260 brass sheet?**  
  The main standard is ASTM B36/B36M, which specifies requirements for cartridge brass sheet.
