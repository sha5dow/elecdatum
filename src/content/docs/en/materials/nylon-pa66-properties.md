## Chemical composition
Nylon PA66 is synthesized from two monomers of 6 carbon atoms each, in a 1:1 stoichiometric ratio of hexamethylenediamine and adipic acid. Its repeating formula and typical elemental composition are shown below.

| Component | Formula | Mass concentration (%) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Base polymer | (C₁₂H₂₂N₂O₂)ₙ | 100 |
| Carbon (C) | — | 63.7 |
| Oxygen (O) | — | 14.2 |
| Nitrogen (N) | — | 12.4 |
| Hydrogen (H) | — | 9.7 |

## Chemical resistance
PA66 exhibits good resistance to hydrocarbons, greases, and common solvents at moderate temperatures, but is attacked by strong mineral acids, concentrated alkaline solutions, and hot oxidizing agents. The following table summarizes general compatibility at 23 °C.

| Chemical agent | Resistance at 23 °C |
| --- | --- |
| Mineral oils | Excellent |
| Gasoline / diesel | Good (slight swelling) |
| Alcohols (ethanol, isopropanol) | Acceptable |
| Ketones (acetone) | Good |
| Weak acids (diluted acetic acid) | Limited |
| Strong acids (HCl, H₂SO₄) | Not recommended |
| Strong bases (10 % NaOH) | Not recommended |
| Hot water (>60 °C) | Limited (hydrolysis) |

## Processing
The processing temperature of Nylon PA66 in injection molding typically ranges from 260 °C to 290 °C. Before processing, the material must be dried to a residual moisture content below 0.20 % to avoid hydrolytic degradation.

| Process parameter | Recommended value |
| --- | --- |
| Drying | 80 °C for 4 h (dehumidified air) |
| Molding temperature (nozzle) | 270 – 290 °C |
| Mold temperature | 60 – 90 °C |
| Injection speed | Medium |
| Holding pressure | 50 – 80 MPa |
| Mold shrinkage | 1.5 % (longitudinal) |
| Extrusion temperature | 260 – 280 °C |

## Design considerations
The moisture absorption of PA66 can reach 8.5 % at saturation, causing a volume increase of approximately 0.2 % per 1 % absorbed moisture. This phenomenon must be compensated for in the design of precision parts by adequate dimensional clearances, glass fiber reinforcements (which reduce expansion to 0.1 % per 1 % moisture), or preconditioning of the components. Mold shrinkage (1.5 %) also requires minimum draft angles of 1° and uniform wall thicknesses to minimize warpage.

## Sustainability and recycling
Nylon PA66 is identified with recycling code 66 and can be reprocessed 3 to 4 times without significant loss of mechanical properties, as long as it is blended with virgin material in controlled proportions (up to 30 % recycled content). It is not biodegradable, but there are mechanical recovery routes (grinding and re‑extrusion) and chemical recycling (depolymerization to recover caprolactam and adipic acid) that are in industrial scale‑up phases. The renewable content in bio‑based grades can reach 60 % when using plant‑based adipic acid.
