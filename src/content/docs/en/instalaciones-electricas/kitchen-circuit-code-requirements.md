## Small Appliance Circuits

Two or more 20 A, 120 V branch circuits must supply all wall, floor, countertop, and refrigerator receptacles in a dwelling's kitchen, pantry, breakfast room, and dining room, in accordance with 210.52(B) and 210.11(C)(1) of the NEC. These circuits may not serve lighting outlets or receptacles located outside those areas. As an exception, a single 15 A or larger circuit is permitted for a specific appliance, such as a refrigerator.

| Requirement | Value |
|---|---|
| Minimum number of circuits | 2 branch circuits / 2 branch circuits |
| Rated voltage and current | 120 V / 120 V and 20 A / 20 A |
| Areas to be covered | Kitchen, pantry, breakfast room, dining room |
| Exception for refrigerator | Single circuit of 15 A / 15 A or larger |

## Wall Receptacle Spacing for Kitchens

No point along the floor line of any kitchen wall may be more than 1.8 m / 6 ft from a receptacle, measured horizontally. Any wall section 0.6 m / 2 ft or more in width, uninterrupted by doors, fireplaces, or fixed cabinets without a countertop, is considered wall space. Floor receptacles located 457 mm / 18 in from the wall count toward the required number; those located more than 1.68 m / 5½ ft above the floor are not counted.

| Parameter | Measurement |
|---|---|
| Maximum horizontal spacing between receptacles | 1.8 m / 6 ft |
| Minimum wall width requiring a receptacle | 0.6 m / 2 ft |
| Maximum distance for counting floor receptacles | 457 mm / 18 in from the wall |
| Maximum height above floor to count as a wall receptacle | 1.68 m / 5½ ft |

## Countertop Receptacle Requirements

Countertop and work surfaces of width 305 mm / 12 in or greater must have receptacles. The maximum horizontal spacing between them is 0.6 m / 2 ft, so that no point on the countertop line is farther from a receptacle. They may be mounted on or above the countertop, provided the height does not exceed 508 mm / 20 in from the surface. Receptacles are not required directly behind a range, countertop-mounted cooking unit, or sink, unless the space behind them exceeds the dimensions indicated in Exception 1 of NEC 210.52(C)(1).

| Requirement | Measurement |
|---|---|
| Minimum countertop width needing a receptacle | 305 mm / 12 in |
| Maximum horizontal spacing between receptacles | 0.6 m / 2 ft |
| Maximum height above countertop | 508 mm / 20 in |

## Overcurrent Protection for Kitchen Equipment (MOCP and MCA)

The MOCP (Maximum Over‑Current Protection) defines the maximum rating of the fuse or circuit breaker that protects equipment, while the MCA (Minimum Circuit Ampacity) sets the minimum current capacity that the conductors must withstand. Both are calculated from the full-load current (FLA) of the motor and resistive loads. The general formula for MOCP is:

> **MOCP = (2,25 × FLA of the largest motor) + other motor loads + resistive loads**

The obtained value is rounded down to the nearest standard size if not a multiple of 5; if the result is less than the MCA, it is set equal to the MCA and rounded up. No breaker shall be less than 15 A / 15 A. As an example, for a refrigerator with a 5 A compressor and a 0.5 A heater: MCA = 1,25 × (5 + 0.5) = 6,875 A, and MOCP = (2,25 × 5) + 0,5 = 11,75 A → a 15 A / 15 A breaker is selected.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How many 20 A circuits are required as a minimum for small appliances in a residential kitchen?
   At least two 20 A, 120 V branch circuits must supply the receptacles in the kitchen, pantry, breakfast room, and dining room per NEC 210.52(B)(1).

### What is the maximum allowed distance between receptacles on a kitchen wall?
   No point along the floor line may be more than 1.8 m / 6 ft from a receptacle.

### What is the maximum height a receptacle may be placed above the countertop?
   Maximum 508 mm / 20 in measured from the countertop surface, per 210.52(C)(5).

### Is it mandatory to install receptacles behind a range or sink?
   They are not required directly behind a range, cooking unit, or sink if the space behind them is smaller than indicated in Exception 1 of 210.52(C)(1); if the space is larger, a GFCI-protected receptacle must be installed.

### What is the minimum MOCP that can be installed on a refrigerator circuit in the kitchen?
   After calculation, it is rounded to the nearest standard size, with a minimum value of 15 A / 15 A.

### Which areas must be covered by small appliance circuits?
   Kitchen, pantry, breakfast room, and dining room; these circuits are not allowed to supply lighting or receptacles outside those zones.
