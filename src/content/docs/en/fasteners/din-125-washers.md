Flat washers according to DIN 125 (forms A and B) are standardized fasteners that distribute the load of bolts and nuts. Version A lacks a chamfer, while version B has a slight chamfer on the inner and outer edges. Below are the standard dimensions, international equivalents and technical data relevant for selection.

## Thickness and surface finish

DIN 125 A washers (without chamfer) have thicknesses ranging from 0.3 mm / 0.012 in for the smallest diameter to 5 mm / 0.197 in for large sizes. Version B, with chamfer, shares the same basic dimensions but requires a better edge finish. Typical surface finishes include bare steel (natural), zinc plated blue, zinc plated yellow, black-oxidized, hot dip galvanized and passivated for A2/A4 stainless steels.

## Torque values

DIN 125 flat washers do not alter the torque values. The tightening torque depends exclusively on the bolt, nut and friction coefficient of the system. For metric fasteners consult ISO 898-1 or DIN 13, and for unified fasteners refer to standard UNC/UNF torque tables.

## Drill sizes

No specific drill is required for washers. The clearance hole must be slightly larger than the bolt outer diameter, typically between 0.2 mm / 0.008 in and 0.5 mm / 0.020 in above the nominal diameter, depending on the desired fit grade.

## Allowable loads

The compressive load capacity of a flat washer is calculated as the product of the effective bearing area and the allowable stress of the material. For standard steel with yield strength ≈ 240 MPa / 34 810 psi and safety factor 1.5, the design stress is 160 MPa / 23 200 psi. The following values are approximate.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. What is the inner diameter of a DIN 125 washer for M8 bolt?**  
The minimum inner diameter is 8.4 mm / 0.331 in, sufficient to accommodate an M8 bolt with standard clearance.

**2. What thickness does a DIN 125 A washer have for metric 12?**  
The standard thickness is 2.5 mm / 0.098 in, both for the version without chamfer and for form B.

**3. Up to what load can an M16 carbon steel washer work?**  
With steel of 240 MPa / 34 810 psi yield strength and safety factor 1.5, it supports approximately 76.8 kN / 17 260 lbf in compression.

**4. Which ISO standard corresponds to DIN 125 A?**  
It corresponds directly to ISO 7089, which specifies flat washers without chamfer for metric fasteners.

**5. With what type of bolts are DIN 125 washers used?**  
They are commonly used with hexagonal bolts, socket head cap screws or metric Allen screws per ISO, and are also compatible with UNC/UNF fasteners if the inner diameter allows.

**6. What constructive difference is there between DIN 125 A and DIN 125 B?**  
Version B incorporates a small chamfer on the inner and outer diameter (bevel of ~0.2‑0.5 mm / 0.008‑0.020 in), while version A has sharp edges.
