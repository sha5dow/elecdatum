## Introduction

MechDatum ("we", "the site") respects the privacy of its visitors. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what choices you have.

## Information We Collect

### Automatic Browsing Data

Like most websites, MechDatum may automatically collect technical information when you visit the site, including:

- IP address
- Browser and device type
- Pages visited and time spent
- Referring site (where you came from)

### Cookies

MechDatum may use first-party and third-party cookies to improve the browsing experience, analyze site traffic, and, where applicable, display relevant advertising through services such as Google AdSense.

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site. Google's use of the DART advertising cookie enables it to serve ads based on your visits to MechDatum and other sites on the internet. Users may opt out of the use of the DART cookie by visiting the [Google Ad and Content Network privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## Use of Information

The information collected is used to:

- Improve the site's content and functionality
- Analyze usage patterns and traffic
- Display relevant advertising (where applicable)

MechDatum may use third-party services that independently collect information, including:

These services have their own privacy policies, which we recommend reviewing.

You can configure your browser to reject cookies or notify you when a cookie is sent. Note that some parts of the site may not function correctly without cookies enabled.
