XHHW cable is identified by its cross-linked polyethylene (XLPE) insulation that provides a maximum operating temperature of 90 °C in dry and wet conditions. The XHHW-2 designation extends the temperature rating to 90 °C in both dry and wet environments, meeting National Electrical Code requirements for indoor, outdoor, and direct burial installations. The internal conductor dimensions are governed by the American Wire Gauge (AWG) standard, where a larger gauge number corresponds to a smaller diameter.

## Material Composition and Properties

The insulation is composed of cross-linked polyethylene (XLPE), a thermoset material that offers resistance to moisture, heat, abrasion, and most chemical compounds. The ability to withstand temperatures up to 90 °C in wet environments distinguishes XHHW from other insulation types, as the "W" rating signifies suitability for use in wet locations. The outer jacket, when present, is generally made of sunlight-resistant and oil-resistant polyvinyl chloride (PVC).

## Technical Specifications

The maximum operating temperature of XHHW cable is 90 °C / 194 °F in dry conditions and 90 °C / 194 °F in wet conditions. The conductor is available in copper or aluminum, with the copper variant being the most common for small and medium gauges.

Insulation thickness varies according to gauge and rated voltage. For 600 V cables, typical XLPE insulation thickness values range from 0,76 mm / 0.030 in for gauges 14 to 10 AWG and 1,14 mm / 0.045 in for gauges 8 to 2 AWG.

## Comparison with Other Insulations

XHHW insulation offers advantages over thermoplastic THHN/THWN in applications requiring greater moisture resistance and higher temperatures in wet environments. While THHN is limited to 75 °C in wet locations, XHHW maintains its dielectric integrity at 90 °C under the same conditions. The thermoset nature of XLPE also provides superior resistance to cold flow and deformation under load.

## Typical Applications

XHHW cable is used in service entrances, feeder circuits, and branch circuits in commercial and industrial buildings. It is suitable for installation in cable trays, conduits, ducts, and direct burial when approved for such use. It is employed in systems requiring high dielectric reliability against moisture, such as installations in basements, exteriors, and process areas, as well as in motor circuits and distribution panel power supply.
